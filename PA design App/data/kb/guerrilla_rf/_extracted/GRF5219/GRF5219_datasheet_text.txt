FEATURES DESCRIPTION
Gain Flatness < 1 dB
The GRF5219 is a high-gain, three-stage driver amplifier
Excellent Linearity Performance Over Wide Bandwidths targeting 1.8 to 2.2 GHz wireless infrastructure
Flexible Biasing Provides Latitude for Linearity applications. The device delivers up to 30.1 dBm of
Optimization OP1dB, 33.7 dBm of OIP3 and a low noise figure (NF) of
less than 4.1 dB over its targeted band of operation.
107 mA Native Mode Quiescent Current Consumption

designed to operate with a single 5 volt supply voltage
